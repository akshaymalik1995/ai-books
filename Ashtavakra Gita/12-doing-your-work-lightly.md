# Lesson 12: Doing Your Work Lightly

In the last lesson, we saw that you can live your normal life and still be
free. Today let us look at your daily work, and how it feels now.

Think about how work usually feels. There is a heavy weight in it. You think,
"I must do this. It all depends on me. If it goes wrong, I am in trouble." So
you carry the work like a load. Your body works, and your mind is tight and
worried the whole time.

Now let us bring in what you know. You are the witness. So watch your work the
way you watched a thought or a feeling.

See the hands doing the work. See the mind planning. See the body moving from
one task to the next. All of this is happening, and you are watching it
happen. The work goes on. And you, the witness, are calm behind it.

Here is the change. The work still gets done. You do not become lazy. You do
not stop. But the heavy weight comes off. You are not crushed by it anymore,
because you can see that you are not the tight, worried doer. You are the calm
one, watching the work happen.

So you act, but you act lightly. The task is the same. The worry is less. You
do what is in front of you, and you let the witness stay calm underneath.

So here is the idea for today:

**You can still do all your work. But now you do it lightly. You watch the
work happen, instead of being crushed by it.**

Try it in a small way. The next time you work, notice once, "I am watching
this work happen." See if the weight gets a little lighter.

That is enough for today.
