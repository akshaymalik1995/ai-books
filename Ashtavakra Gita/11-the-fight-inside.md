# Lesson 11: The Fight Inside

In the last lessons, you learned to watch. Now let us use this watching on
something hard. Something that troubles almost everyone. Let us look at what
happens when you put off something important. Let us look at when you
procrastinate.

Here is the scene. You have something important to do. A deadline is coming.
It matters to you. You sit down to do it.

But your hand reaches for the phone instead. You start to scroll. One video.
Then another. The important thing sits there, not done.

Now watch what happens inside you. It is not quiet in there. It is loud. Let
us listen.

One voice says, "What is wrong with you? You always do this. You are going to
fail. You have no discipline." This voice is hard and angry. It is blaming
you.

Another voice says, "Okay. Five more minutes. Then I will really start." This
voice is trying to fix it. It is pushing you.

And under both of them, there is the part that just wants to escape. The part
that keeps reaching for the phone.

So look at how many things are happening at once. One part wants to escape.
One part is blaming. One part is pushing. And they are not friends. They are
fighting each other.

See it clearly. The blaming voice attacks the escaping part. The pushing
voice fights the escaping part. Inside you, one part of you is at war with
another part of you.

This is the thing almost no one notices. You think procrastination is one
simple problem. You think, "I am just not doing the work." But that is not
the real trouble. The real trouble is the fight. All through those hours, one
part of you is attacking another part of you. And it hurts.

And notice one more thing. The fight does not even work. The blaming does not
get you off the phone. The pushing does not get you off the phone. You blame
and push for one hour, two hours, and you are still scrolling. The war goes
on, and the work still does not get done.

So here is the idea for today:

**When you procrastinate, the real trouble is not only the work you are not
doing. Inside you, one part is fighting another part. And that fight is where
the pain is.**

For today, do not try to fix anything. Do not try to win the fight. Just see
that there is a fight. See the one who escapes. See the one who blames. See
the one who pushes. Just notice, "There is a war going on inside me."

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will turn to the loudest fighter of all. The one who
blames you. And we will ask a strange question about it. Is that blaming
voice really you?
