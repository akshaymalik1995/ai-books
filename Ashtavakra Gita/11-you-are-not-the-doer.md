# Lesson 11: You Are Not the Doer

In the last lessons, you learned to watch. You are the witness. You watch the
body. You watch the thoughts. You watch the feelings. Today Ashtavakra says
something that sounds strange. Stay with me, and go slow.

He says you are not the doer. You are not the one who does things.

He says it in a strong way. The idea "I am the doer" is like a poison in you.
It has made you suffer for a long time. The cure is one simple thought: "I am
not the doer." Take that in, he says, and be happy.

At first this sounds wrong. You think, "Of course I am the doer. I walk. I
work. I speak. I did all of that." But let us look.

Start with the easy ones. Your heart is beating right now. Are you doing that?
No. It beats on its own. You breathe all day and all night. You are not doing
it. It happens by itself. You grew from a baby into a grown person. You did
not do that either. It just happened.

So much of "your" doing is not done by you at all. It happens on its own. And
you watch it.

Now take the things you call yours. You walk across the room. The legs move.
The body goes. You are aware of it. The walking happens, and you see it. Now
look for a "doer" inside you, one who makes the walking happen. You will not
find one. There is just walking. And there is you, watching it.

But a feeling comes and says, "I am the one doing this." That feeling brings
all the weight. "It all rests on me. I must push. I must control." Let that
feeling go. The doing still happens. You just stop carrying it.

So here is the idea for today:

**You are not the doer. The body and the mind act on their own. You are the
one who watches. You do not have to carry it.**

Let me be clear, so this is not misread. It does not mean nothing gets done.
Things get done all day. It means you are not the one who has to strain and
push. The doing happens. You watch. And a great weight falls away.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will take this into a hard, real case. It is the case
where you put off something important, and blame yourself, and push yourself.
We will see what "I am not the doer" looks like right there.
