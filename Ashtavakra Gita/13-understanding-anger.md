# Lesson 13: Understanding Anger

Let us take everything we have learned and apply it to another hard situation. One that can take hold of us very quickly. Anger.

Here is the scene. Someone says something unkind to you. Or they do the very thing you asked them not to do. And almost at once, before you can even think, anger rises up inside you. Your body grows tense. Your heart beats faster. And a voice comes, quick and sure.

*"How can they do this to me? They have no right. I will not let this go."*

In that moment, there is no space at all. You feel as if you *are* the anger. It has filled you completely, and it wants to act. It wants to say the sharp word, or hit back, or make the other person feel it too.

Now let us bring in what you have learned. This anger, as strong as it feels, is only a movement of the mind. It rose up on its own. You did not sit down and choose it. And if it came on its own, then it is not you. It is something you can watch.

So try this, even for one breath. Instead of being the anger, simply notice it.

*"Anger is here."*

Feel it, and watch it. You are the one who sees the tense body, the fast heart, the quick voice. And the one who sees the anger is not angry. The anger is in the mind. You are the quiet one, watching it.

Something gentle happens in that small moment of watching. A little space opens. A bit of room between you and the anger. And in that space, you are free. The anger is still there, but it is no longer in charge. You do not have to become it. You do not have to act on it.

Let me be honest with you. This does not mean the anger will go away, or that you will never feel it again. It will still come. But when you can watch it instead of being it, it no longer moves your hands and your words for you. It rises, you watch, and it passes. And you did not do the thing you would have been sorry for.

And notice one more thing. You are not fighting the anger either. You are not pushing it down, or telling yourself that you should not feel it. That is only another struggle. You are simply seeing it. Watching, not fighting, is what sets you free.

So here is today's idea:

**Anger is a movement of the mind. It is not you. When it comes, do not become it, and do not fight it. Just watch it. In that watching, a little space opens, and you are free to let it pass.**

The next time anger rises in you, do not rush to act, and do not scold yourself for feeling it. Just watch. *"Anger is here."* See it clearly. And notice the quiet one who is watching. That one is you. And that one was never angry at all.

That is enough for today.
