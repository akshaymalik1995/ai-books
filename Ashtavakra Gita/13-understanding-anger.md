# Lesson 13: Understanding Anger

Let us take everything we have learned and apply it to another hard situation — one that can grip us in a single second. Anger.

Here is the scene. Someone says something sharp to you. Or they do the very thing you asked them not to do. And before you can even think, something rises up inside you, fast and hot. Your chest tightens. Your face grows warm. And a voice comes, quick and certain.

*"How dare they. They can't treat me like this. I'll show them."*

In that moment, there is no space at all. You *are* the anger. It has taken you over completely, and it wants to act — to say the harsh thing, to strike back, to make them feel it too.

Now let us bring in what you have learned. That anger, however hot it burns, is a movement of the mind. It rose up on its own. You did not sit down and choose it. And if it came on its own, then it is not you. It is something you can see.

So try this, even for a single breath. Instead of being the anger, notice it.

*"Anger is here."*

Feel it burn, and watch it burn. You are the one who sees the heat, the tight chest, the racing voice. And the one who sees the anger is not angry. The anger is in the mind. You are the quiet one, watching it.

Something happens in that small moment of watching. A little space opens — a breath of room between you and the anger. And in that space, you are free. The anger is still there, but it is no longer driving. You do not have to become it. You do not have to act it out.

Let me be honest with you. This does not mean the anger will disappear, or that you will never feel it again. It will still come. But when you can see it instead of being it, it no longer moves your hands and your mouth for you. It rises, you watch, and it passes — and you did not do the thing you would have regretted.

And notice one more thing. You are not fighting the anger either. You are not pushing it down, or telling yourself you should not feel it. That is only another battle. You are simply seeing it. Watching, not fighting, is what sets you free.

So here is today's idea:

**Anger is a movement of the mind. It is not you. When it rises, do not become it, and do not fight it — just see it. In that seeing, a little space opens, and you are free to let it pass.**

The next time the heat rises in you, do not rush to act, and do not scold yourself for feeling it. Just watch. *"Anger is here."* See it burn. And notice the quiet one who is watching. That one is you — and that one was never angry at all.

That is enough for today.
