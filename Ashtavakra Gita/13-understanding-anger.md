# Lesson 13: Understanding Anger

Let us take everything we have learned and apply it to something that can rise in us very quickly: anger.

Imagine this. Someone says something unkind to you. Or they do the very thing you asked them not to do. Almost at once, before you can even think, anger rises up inside you. Your body grows tense. Your heartbeat gets faster.

And a voice appears in your mind.

*"How can they do this to me? They have no right. I will not let this go."*

In that moment, there is no space. You feel as if you are the anger itself. It fills you, and it wants to act. It wants to say something harsh, or hit back, or make the other person feel it too.

Now bring in what you have learned. This anger, as strong as it feels, is only a movement of the mind. It rose up on its own. You did not choose it. And if it came on its own, then it is not you. It is something you can watch.

So try this, even for a moment. Instead of being the anger, simply notice it.

*"Anger is here."*

Feel it, and watch it. You are the one who notices the tense body, the fast heartbeat, the words forming in the mind. And the one who watches the anger is not angry. The anger is in the mind. You are the quiet one watching it.

In that small moment of watching, something changes. A little space opens between you and the anger. And in that space, you are free. The anger is still there, but it is no longer in charge. You do not have to become it, and you do not have to act on it.

Let me be honest with you. This does not mean the anger will disappear, or that you will never feel it again. It will still come. But when you can watch it instead of being it, it no longer moves your hands and your words for you. It rises, you watch, and it passes. And you did not do the thing you would have been sorry for.

Notice one more thing. You are not fighting the anger either. You are not pushing it down, or telling yourself you should not feel it. That is only another struggle. You are simply seeing it. Watching, not fighting, is what sets you free.

So here is today's idea:

**Anger is a movement of the mind. It is not you. When it comes, do not become it, and do not fight it. Just watch it. In that watching, a little space opens, and you are free to let it pass.**

The next time anger rises in you, do not rush to act, and do not blame yourself for feeling it. Just watch. *"Anger is here."* And notice the quiet one who is watching. That one is you. And that one was never angry at all.

That is enough for today.
