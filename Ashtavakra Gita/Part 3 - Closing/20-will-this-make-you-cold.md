# Lesson 20: Will This Make You Cold?

There is a fear that often comes to a person learning all of this. Perhaps it has come to you already.

It goes something like this: *"If I keep watching my feelings, if I keep seeing that the anger and the worry and the wanting are not me — won't I slowly go cold? Won't I stop caring? Won't I become distant, and empty, and hard?"*

It is a good fear to bring into the open. Because if this teaching truly made people cold, it would not be worth much.

So let me say it plainly. It will not make you cold. It does the very opposite. But we have to be careful here, because two very different things can look alike from far away.

One is numbness. Numbness is pushing your feelings down, refusing to feel them, closing your heart so that nothing can reach you. It is a hard, tight, lonely way to live. And it is not what we have been doing. Not once have we said, "Do not feel." We have said the opposite. Feel the anger. Feel the grief. Let it be here. Turn toward it; do not run from it.

The other is freedom. Freedom is feeling everything fully, and no longer being ruled by it. You still feel the anger, but it no longer makes you cruel. You still feel the grief, but it no longer destroys you. You still feel love — and you feel it more, because you are no longer so lost in your own worries that you miss the person right in front of you.

Do you see the difference? Numbness feels less. Freedom feels fully, and is simply not owned by what it feels.

Think of the people you have known who seemed truly at peace. Perhaps an old grandmother, or a quiet friend, or a teacher who always had time for you. Were they cold? Almost never. They were usually the warmest people you knew. Because they were not caught up in their own troubles, they had room for you. Their peace did not close their heart. It opened it.

That is what this is. When you are no longer lost in your own anger and fear and wanting, you do not become empty. You become free. And a free heart does not love less. It loves more easily, because it is not so afraid, and not so busy protecting itself.

There is one wrong turn to watch for. Some people take this teaching and use it to stop caring — *"nothing matters, none of it is real, none of it is me."* That is not freedom. It is only hiding, and calling it wisdom. The real thing does not pull you back from life. It sets you down in the middle of it, awake, and able to care without being crushed.

So do not worry. You are not going to fade into some cold, grey calm. You are going to become more yourself, not less. Warmer, not colder. More here, not further away.

The quiet awareness that you are is not the opposite of love. It is the very thing that lets you love at all.
