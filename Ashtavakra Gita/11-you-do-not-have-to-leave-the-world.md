# Lesson 11: You Do Not Have to Leave the World

In the last lesson, we learned the simple practice of seeing. Today let us
clear up a common mistake.

When people hear all this, many of them think one thing. They think, "To be
free, I must leave the world. I must give up my job. I must leave my home and
my family. I must go far away and sit alone."

This is not true. Let me show you why.

Remember who Ashtavakra was teaching. He was teaching Janaka. And Janaka was
a king. He had a kingdom to run. He had work all day. He had people to care
for. Ashtavakra did not tell him to leave the kingdom. He told him to see the
truth inside. Janaka stayed a king. And he became free.

Think about what we have learned. You are the witness. The witness is inside
you. It is not in some far away place. So the freedom is inside you too. You
carry it with you wherever you go. You do not have to travel to reach it. You
do not have to leave anything to find it.

So you can keep your job. You can stay with your family. You can live your
normal life. And in the middle of all of it, you can see that you are the
witness. The outer life stays the same. The inside changes.

So here is the idea for today:

**You do not have to leave the world to be free. The freedom is inside you.
You can live your normal life and still be free.**

Do not wait for the perfect quiet place. Do not wait to give things up. You
can begin right here, right now, in the life you already have.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will look at how this changes your daily work and the
things you do.
