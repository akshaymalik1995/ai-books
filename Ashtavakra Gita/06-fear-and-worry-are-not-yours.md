# Lesson 6: Fear and Worry Are Not Yours

In the last lesson, we found the big thing. You are the witness — the calm one who watches, and does not change.

So now let us ask a real question. What about your fear? What about your worry? They feel so strong. They feel so much like *you*. Where do they fit?

Ashtavakra gives a simple answer. He says that pleasure and pain, and even right and wrong, are all just things happening in the mind. They are not you.

Watch what happens when fear comes. A fear rises up. The mind tightens, and the thoughts start to race. But stop for a moment and look. Who is it that sees the fear?

You do. The witness sees the fear. And we said that the witness does not change. So the fear is happening *in the mind*. It is not happening in you, the witness. The fear is something you see. It is not something you are.

This is a big turn, so go slowly. Before, you would say, "I am afraid." Now you can see it more truly. The mind is afraid, and you are the one watching the afraid mind. You and the fear are not the same thing.

The same is true of worry. Worry comes into the mind, stays a while, and then it goes. And the whole time, you are behind it, watching. The worry moves. You do not. The worry is not yours.

So here is today's idea:

**Fear and worry happen in the mind. They are not you. You are the witness who watches them, and stays calm.**

You do not have to fight the fear. You do not have to push the worry away. Just see that they are not you. See that you are the calm one, watching. That seeing, on its own, is enough to loosen their hold.

That is enough for today.
