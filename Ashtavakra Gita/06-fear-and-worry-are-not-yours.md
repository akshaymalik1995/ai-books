# Lesson 6: Fear and Worry Are Not Yours

> **Ashtavakra Gita 1.6**
> "Righteousness and unrighteousness, pleasure and pain are purely of the mind
> and are no concern of yours. You are neither the doer nor the reaper of the
> consequences, so you are always free."

In the last lesson, we found the big thing. You are the witness. You are the
calm one who watches, and does not change.

Now let us ask a real question. What about your fear? What about your worry?
They feel so strong. They feel like *you*. So where do they fit?

Watch what happens when fear comes. A fear rises. Your mind gets tight. Your
thoughts run fast. But stop and look. Who sees the fear?

You do. The witness sees the fear. And we said the witness does not change.
So the fear is happening *in the mind*. It is not happening in you, the
witness. The fear is a thing you see. It is not a thing you are.

This is a big turn, so go slow. Before, you thought, "I am afraid." Now you
can see it more truly. The mind is afraid. You are the one watching the
afraid mind. You and the fear are not the same.

The same is true for worry. Worry comes into the mind. It stays for a while.
Then it goes. All the time, you are behind it, watching. The worry moves. You
do not move. The worry is not yours.

So here is the idea for today:

**Fear and worry happen in the mind. They are not you. You are the witness
who watches them, and stays calm.**

You do not have to fight the fear. You do not have to push the worry away.
Just see that they are not you. See that you are the calm one, watching. That
seeing is enough to loosen their hold.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will look at how this changes the way you meet your
whole life.
