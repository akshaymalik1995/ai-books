# Lesson 12: An Example — When You Put Things Off

Let us take everything we have learned and use it on one hard, real thing. It
is something that troubles almost everyone — putting off important work.

Here is the scene. You have something important to do, and it matters. You sit
down to do it. But your hand reaches for the phone instead, and you start to
scroll. The important thing just sits there, not done.

Now look at what happens inside you. It is not quiet in there. One voice blames
you. "What is wrong with you? You are going to fail." Then another voice pushes
you. "Come on, force yourself, just five more minutes." And underneath them
both, a part of you simply wants to escape into the phone.

Now remember what Ashtavakra told us. Right and wrong, pleasure and pain, are
only things in the mind. And you are not the doer. So look again at those two
voices. The one that blames you is only the mind judging, so it is not you. The
one that pushes you is only the mind straining, so it is not you either. You
are the one sitting behind them, watching them both.

So the blaming and the pushing are not you trying to fix yourself. They are
just noise. And here is something worth noticing. That noise does not even
work. You have blamed and pushed yourself for years now, and here you still
are, scrolling. So it was never the noise that got you moving.

Then what does get you moving, if not the fight? It is a quiet, clear seeing.
When the blaming and the pushing settle down, you can just see for yourself.
You see that the scrolling is empty right now. You see that the work really
matters to you. And out of that seeing, the doing can begin on its own. You did
not force it. It came the way your breath comes, on its own, while you watch.

So here is the idea for today:

**When you put things off, the blaming and the pushing are not you. They are
just the mind. You are not the doer. So you do not have to win the fight. Let
it grow quiet, and let clear seeing move you.**

So do not try to force anything. The next time you put something off, just
watch the whole thing happen. Watch the part that wants to escape, the part
that blames you, and the part that pushes you. See that not one of them is
really you. That seeing, all on its own, begins to loosen the hold.

That is enough for today.
