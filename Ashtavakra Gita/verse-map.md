# Verse Map (working backbone)

This file is our **guide**, not something the reader sees. It keeps each
lesson tied to the text of the Ashtavakra Gita so the teaching stays true and
does not drift. The lessons themselves are plain, one-idea beginner lessons;
they do not quote the verses. Where a verse fits, a lesson mentions it in plain
words. If a lesson's idea cannot be traced to a verse, that is our signal we
have drifted, and we cut or rework it.

> Verse wording in the table below is a **gist** for quick scanning. The exact
> John Richards text is in the folder PDF and credited in `sources.md`.

---

## Chapter 1 — Ashtavakra's first teaching (20 verses)

| Idea | Verse(s) | Gist |
|------|----------|------|
| A. The question | 1.1 | Janaka asks: how is knowledge gained, liberation attained, dispassion reached? |
| B. Turn from craving | 1.2 | Shun sense-objects like poison; value forgiveness, honesty, kindness, contentment, truth. |
| C. You are not the body; you are the witness | 1.3, 1.4, 1.14 | You are not earth/water/fire/air/space. Know yourself as the witness, awareness — and be free at once. |
| D. Not your role or the senses | 1.5 | You are not of any caste or life-stage, not a seen object; you are the formless witness of all. |
| E. Right/wrong, pleasure/pain are of the mind; you are not the doer | 1.6, 1.8 | Virtue and vice, pleasure and pain, belong to the mind, not you. You are neither doer nor enjoyer. Drop "I am the doer." |
| F. Your only bondage: mistaking yourself for the doer | 1.7 | You are the one witness, ever free; the sole bondage is seeing the witness as something other. |
| G. Freedom is a knowing that ends ignorance | 1.9 | Burn ignorance with the conviction "I am one pure awareness." |
| H. The world appears in you | 1.10, 1.12 | The universe appears in awareness like a snake imagined in a rope; the Self is quiet, actionless. |
| I. As you think, so you become | 1.11 | Think you are free, you are free; think you are bound, you are bound. |
| J. The changeless, actionless Self | 1.12, 1.13, 1.15, 1.16, 1.17 | The Self is all-pervading, one, changeless, actionless, unattached, serene; it seems worldly only through illusion. |
| K. The seen is false, the formless is real | 1.18 | Know form as false, the formless as lasting. |
| L. Within and without | 1.19, 1.20 | The Self is inside and outside the body, as space is inside and outside a jar. |

## Current lessons (the verse that guides each)

A ✓ marks a lesson that mentions its verse in plain words; the rest are simply
guided by it, with no mention.

- L1 You want to be free → **1.1** ✓ (Janaka's question)
- L2 What stands in the way → **1.7**
- L3 You are not your body → **1.3** ✓ (not earth, water, fire, air)
- L4 You are not your mind → guided by the witness idea (1.3, 1.5); no clean verse — teach plainly
- L5 You are the witness → **1.12** ✓ (he names the witness)
- L6 Fear and worry are not yours → **1.6** ✓ (pleasure and pain are of the mind)
- L7 Life keeps changing, you do not → **1.6**
- L8 You are already free → **1.15** ✓ (already unbound)
- L9 Why you do not feel free yet → **1.14** ✓ (long trapped in the body)
- L10 How to see → **1.4**
- L11 You are not the doer → **1.8** ✓ (the snakebite "I am the doer")
- L12 An example — when you put things off → **1.6 / 1.8** ✓

The old procrastination arc (five free-standing lessons) has been compressed
into **one example lesson, L12**, after a lesson on "you are not the doer"
(L11).
