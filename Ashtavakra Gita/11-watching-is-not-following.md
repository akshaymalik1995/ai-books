# Lesson 11: Watching Is Not Following

In the last lesson, I asked you to just watch. To stop controlling, and only
see. But this is easy to hear in a wrong way. So today we clear it up. This
is an important one, so go slow.

When I say "stop controlling, just watch," you may think something like this.
"I understand now. I am only the witness. So I do not need to do anything. I
will just let my mind do whatever it wants. Any thought that comes, I will go
along with it. That is letting go."

This sounds right. But it is not what watching means. Let me show you the
difference, because it is the whole thing.

There are two wrong ways with the mind. And there is one true way.

The first wrong way is the old way. You fight the mind. You push it. You try
to force it to be quiet. This is control. It is tiring, and it does not work.

The second wrong way is the opposite. You give up. You say, "I am just the
witness, so I will let the mind do anything." Now the mind runs wherever it
likes, and you go along with it. A thought says "worry," so you worry. A
thought says "be angry," so you get angry. You call this letting go. But look
closely. The mind is now in charge, and you are just following it.

Do you see the two? In the first way, you push the mind. In the second way,
the mind pushes you. Both are a kind of being tied. Neither one is free.

Now the true way. To watch is neither of these. When a thought comes, you do
not fight it. And you do not follow it. You just see it. You let it be there,
and you do not move with it.

Look at the difference with care. A thought of anger comes. If you follow it,
you become angry. The same thought comes, and if you truly watch it, you see
the anger, but you are not pulled in. You stay as the calm witness. The
thought is there. But it does not take you.

So being the witness does not mean you let the mind do anything. It does not
mean you go along with every thought. It means the very opposite. It means
you are no longer pushed by the mind at all.

So here is the idea for today:

**To watch the mind is not to follow the mind. Being the witness does not
mean you let your mind do whatever it wants. It means you see each thought,
and you do not get pulled by it.**

Try to feel this line clearly. Following a thought and watching a thought are
not the same. One pulls you in. The other leaves you free.

That is enough for today. This is a fine line, so we will look at it once more
in the next lesson, in a real and simple way.
