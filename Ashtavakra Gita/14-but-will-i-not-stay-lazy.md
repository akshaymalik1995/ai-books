# Lesson 14: But Will I Not Stay Lazy?

We have seen two voices inside you. One criticizes you, hard. The other
pushes you to do better. And we saw that neither of them is you. Now, a fear
may come up. It is a strong fear, and many people feel it here. Let us meet it
today, straight on.

The fear says this. "If I stop criticizing myself, and if I lose the voice
that pushes me, then what is left? Without the criticism and without the
push, nothing will make me work. I will just sit and do nothing. I will
scroll forever. I will lose everything."

This fear feels very real. It feels like your self-criticism and your
motivation are the only things holding your life up. Take them away, and you
fall. That is what the fear is sure of.

I want to take this fear seriously. So let us not answer it with a nice idea.
Let us answer it with your own experience. Let us look slowly, and really
check.

Inside the fear there is a belief. The belief is this: "My criticism and my
motivation are what make me work. Without them, I would do nothing." This
belief feels obvious. But we are going to look at it closely. Because your
whole fear stands on it.

Let us start with one real time you put something off. Bring it to mind. You
were scrolling. And the criticism was there the whole time. "You are wasting
your life. You are going to fail." And the push to do better was there too.
"Come on, start now, force yourself." These voices were not quiet. They were
loud. For one hour. For two hours. For three.

Now here is a simple question. If they worked, when would you have stopped? In
the first minute. The criticism and the push were at full strength from the
start. If they had the power to move you, you would have moved at once. But
you did not. They were loud for three hours, and you scrolled for three hours.
All that noise, and it did not lift your hand from the phone even once.

Maybe you want to say, "But sometimes I do finally start." That is true. So
let us look at that moment too, honestly. When you finally started, what
really changed? Most times, the deadline had come so close that everything
changed. Or you suddenly got interested. Or you were simply tired of
scrolling. But the pushing had been the same all day. It did not grow stronger
at the last minute. Something else changed. So it was not the push that
finally moved you. It just happened to be there when something else did the
work.

Now step back and see the biggest proof of all. You do not criticize and push
yourself once in a while. You do it all the time. Day after day. Year after
year. So ask yourself this. If self-criticism and pushing were the thing that
makes a person act, then a person who does it as much as you do would be the
busiest, most active person alive. But you are not. You still put things off.
You have criticized yourself for years, and here you still are, scrolling.
That long history is your proof. The criticism does not make the action.

And now look at the other side. Think of a time when you did work well. When
it flowed, and you were fully in it, and the hours passed with ease. Were you
criticizing yourself in that time? Were you pushing hard against yourself?
Almost never. In those good times, the harsh voice and the push were quiet.
You had forgotten them. The good, easy work came when they were silent, not
when they were loud. That is the very opposite of what the fear tells you.

So put it all together.

**The fear says your self-criticism and your push to do better are the only
things that make you work. But look honestly at your own life. They are loud
all day and you still do not move. When you do move, something else moves you.
And your best work comes when they are quiet. They were never the thing that
made you act.**

So letting them go takes away nothing that was helping you. This does not mean
you will do nothing. It means the thing you were so scared to lose was never
doing the job at all.

That is enough for today.

But now an honest question is left. If the criticism and the push were not the
thing making me act, then what does? Where does real doing come from? That is
what we will look at in the next lesson.
