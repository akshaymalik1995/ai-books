# Lesson 7: Life Keeps Changing, You Do Not

In the last lesson, we saw that fear and worry are not you. They come into the mind, and they pass. Today we take that same idea and make it larger. We take it to your whole life.

Look at your life. It is always changing. A good day comes, and then a hard day comes. You get something you wanted, and then you lose something you loved. People come close, and people drift away. Nothing stays the same for very long.

Most people rise and fall along with all of it. When something good arrives, they lift up. When something bad arrives, they sink down. Up and down, up and down, their whole life through. There is no rest in it.

But now you know something new. You are the witness, and the witness does not change. So watch what that means.

The good day comes, and you see it. The hard day comes, and you see that too. Both of them pass in front of you, and you are the one watching them pass. You do not have to rise and fall with each one. You can stay still, and watch them come and go.

This does not mean you stop living. You still work. You still care. You still feel things deeply. But underneath it all, there is a calm place that does not move. And that calm place is you — the witness.

So here is today's idea:

**Your life keeps changing. But you, the witness, do not change. So you can meet all of it, and still be at peace.**

Good days and hard days will always come and go. You do not have to be pulled around by them. You can watch them pass, and rest as the quiet one who stays.

That is enough for today.
