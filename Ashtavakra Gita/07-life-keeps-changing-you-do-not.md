# Lesson 7: Life Keeps Changing, You Do Not

> **Ashtavakra Gita 1.6**
> *Ashtavakra:* "Righteousness and unrighteousness, pleasure and pain are purely of the mind
> and are no concern of yours. You are neither the doer nor the reaper of the
> consequences, so you are always free."

In the last lesson, we saw that fear and worry are not you. They come into
the mind, and they pass. Today we take that same idea and make it bigger. We
take it to your whole life.

Look at your life. It is always changing. A good day comes. Then a hard day
comes. You get something you wanted. Then you lose something you liked.
People come close. People go away. Nothing stays the same for long.

Most people go up and down with all of this. When a good thing comes, they
rise. When a bad thing comes, they fall. Up and down, up and down, all their
life. There is no rest.

But now you know something new. You are the witness. And the witness does not
change. So watch what this means.

The good day comes. You see it. The hard day comes. You see it too. Both of
them pass in front of you. You are the one watching them pass. You do not
have to rise and fall with each one. You can stay still and see them come and
go.

This does not mean you stop living. You still work. You still care. You still
feel. But underneath it all, there is a calm place that does not move. That
calm place is you, the witness.

So here is the idea for today:

**Your life keeps changing. But you, the witness, do not change. So you can
meet all of it and still be at peace.**

Good days and bad days will always come and go. You do not have to be pulled
by them. You can watch them pass, and rest as the quiet one who stays.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will look at a question that may be growing in you by
now. If I am this calm witness, then what was all my seeking for?
