# Lesson 21: Where You Go From Here

We have come a long way together, you and I. Before we part, let me gather it up.

You came here with one wish. You wanted to be free. That was the very first thing we said.

And slowly, we found something surprising. The freedom you were looking for was not far away, and it was not something to earn. What you truly are — the quiet awareness that has been reading every one of these words — was already free. It always had been. You did not have to become anything. You only had to see what was already true.

Then we learned to see it, gently, in small moments. And we walked together through the hard places — the worry at night, the anger, the comparing, the grief, the wanting — and in each one we found the same quiet thing, waiting underneath.

Now, here is what I want you to know as you go.

You will not remember all of this. You do not need to. If you forget every lesson but one small thing, let it be this: in any moment, you can turn and notice the one who is aware. That noticing is the whole path. Everything else was only pointing at it.

And this is not something you finish. It is not a test you pass and set down. It is something you live, one quiet moment at a time. You will forget it for days. You will get pulled back into the worry and the anger and the wanting, again and again. That is completely alright. There is no failing here. The moment you remember, you are already home. You were never really gone.

So be gentle with yourself. Do not turn this into one more thing to be good at. Come back to whichever lesson you need, on whichever day you need it. The book will wait for you.

Remember the king we began with. Janaka did not leave his throne. He did not run off to a cave. He lived his whole full life — the work, the troubles, the people, the noise — and he was free through all of it. You can do the same, right in the middle of your ordinary days. You do not have to change your life. You only have to know who is living it.

And that quiet awareness that you are — it was here before you opened this book. It is here now, as you read the last of it. And it will be here for the rest of your life, closer to you than your own breath.

You were always free. Now you know.

Go gently.
