# Lesson 5: You Are the Witness

Let us bring the last two lessons together.

You are not your body — you watch your body. You are not your mind — you watch your thoughts and your feelings.

So in both cases, there was someone there, watching. Today we turn and look at that watcher.

Ashtavakra has a name for it. He calls it the *witness*. A witness is someone who sees a thing happen. The witness does not do the thing. The witness only watches.

This is what you are. You are the one who watches. You watch the body move. You watch thoughts arrive and leave. You watch feelings rise and fall. And through all of it, you are the quiet one who simply sees.

Notice something about this witness. It does not change. The body changes, the thoughts change, the feelings change — but the one who sees them does not. It was here when you were a child. It is here right now. It has been the same witness the whole time.

And notice one more thing. The witness is always calm. A sad thought is sad, yes. But the one who *sees* the sad thought is not sad. It only watches. The trouble is in the mind. The witness stays still, and simply sees it.

So here is today's idea:

**You are the witness. You are the calm one who watches everything, and never changes.**

This is who you really are. You are not the body. You are not the mind. You are the quiet witness behind them both.

That is enough for today. Sit with this one for a while. It is the heart of the whole book.

In the next lesson, we ask what this means for you. If you are the calm witness, then what happens to all your fear and worry?
