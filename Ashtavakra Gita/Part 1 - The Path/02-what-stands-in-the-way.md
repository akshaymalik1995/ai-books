# Lesson 2: What Stands in the Way

In the last lesson, we said one simple thing. You want to be free.

So now a real question comes up. If you want to be free, why are you not free already? What is actually stopping you?

Most people look outside for the answer. They think, *"I will be free once I have more money. I will be free once this problem is over. I will be free once that person finally changes."*

So they wait. They fix one thing on the outside, and then another, and then another. But the not-free feeling never really leaves. It keeps coming back.

Ashtavakra points in a completely different direction. He says the thing that stops you is not outside you at all. It is inside you. It is a wrong idea in your own mind.

You feel bound because you *think* you are bound. That thought is the only thing holding you. Not the world, not your money, not other people — only a quiet, wrong idea about yourself.

So here is today's idea:

**What stops you from being free is not outside you. It is a wrong idea inside you.**

And this is good news. Something on the outside is hard to change. But an idea can be seen, and it can be set down. If the thing holding you is only an idea, then you are far closer to free than you ever thought.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we begin to look at that wrong idea. We will ask a simple question: who are you, really?
