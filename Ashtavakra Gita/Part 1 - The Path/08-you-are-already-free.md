# Lesson 8: You Are Already Free

Let us look back for a moment. In the very first lesson, you came here with one wish. You wanted to be free. Now we are ready for the biggest turn in the whole book.

Think about everything we have seen. You are not the body. You are not the mind. You are the witness. The witness does not change, and it stays calm while good days and hard days pass.

Now ask one question about this witness. Was it ever bound?

Look, and see for yourself. The witness only watches. It never gets caught. It never gets tied down. When fear came, the witness stayed free. When a hard day came, the witness stayed free. It was never once bound. It was free the entire time.

So here is the surprise. You were searching for freedom. But the one who was searching was already free. You never needed to become free. You only needed to see that you already are.

This is what Ashtavakra shows the king. You are already free. The very thing you are searching for, you already are.

Go slowly here. You thought freedom was somewhere far away. You thought you had to reach it one day. But the witness — the real you — was free from the very start. Freedom is not a place you travel to. It is the thing you already are, right now, as you read these words.

So here is today's idea:

**You do not have to become free. You are already free. You only have to see it.**

Let this settle in you. There is nowhere to go. There is nothing to get. The freedom you were longing for is what you have been all along.

That is enough for today.
