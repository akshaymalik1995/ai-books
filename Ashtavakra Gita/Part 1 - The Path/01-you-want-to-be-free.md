# Lesson 1: You Want to Be Free

Let us begin.

Long ago, there was a king named Janaka. He had everything a person could want. A kingdom, wealth, power — all of it was his. And yet, deep inside, he was not at peace.

So one day he went to a teacher named Ashtavakra, and he asked a single question.

*"How do I become free?"*

That is where this book begins. One man, sitting with his teacher, asking how to be free.

I think you are here for a reason much like his. Something in you wants that too — to be free from worry, free from fear, free from that heavy, sad feeling that keeps coming back no matter what you do.

This whole book is an answer to that one question. Nothing more than that.

So here is the first idea. It is small. Just hold it gently:

**You want to be free. And this book is the answer to that wish.**

You do not need to do anything yet. You do not need to believe anything yet. Just know why you are here. You want peace. You want to be free.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will look at the first thing that stands in the way.
