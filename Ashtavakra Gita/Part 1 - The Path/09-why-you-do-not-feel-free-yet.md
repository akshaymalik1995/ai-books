# Lesson 9: Why You Do Not Feel Free Yet

In the last lesson, we said a big thing. You are already free. But right after hearing it, you may have felt a small doubt rise up.

*"If I am already free, then why do I still not feel free?"*

It is a good and honest question. Ashtavakra gives the reason. For a very long time, you have been caught in a single habit — the habit of thinking, "I am the body."

Look at how old that habit is. Every day, from the time you were small, you thought it. "I am the body. I am the mind. I am bound." You thought it again and again, for years and years, until it became a habit — a very old, very strong habit of the mind.

Now, in just a few lessons, you have seen something new. You have seen that you are the free witness. That seeing is true. But it is new. And the old habit is still there. The old habit is loud, because it is old. The new seeing is quiet, because it is young.

So you are free, and yet you do not feel it yet. Not because the freedom is not real — it is completely real. You do not feel it because the old habit is still covering it over. That is all. An old habit is simply resting on top of the truth.

Hear this clearly. The habit is not you. It is only an old way of thinking. And a habit can fade. Each time you see that you are the witness, the old habit grows a little weaker. The truth was always there. Slowly, you stop covering it.

So here is today's idea:

**You do not feel free yet because of an old habit of thinking you are bound. The habit is not the truth. And it fades as you keep seeing.**

Do not be sad that the feeling has not arrived yet. And do not push for it. Just keep seeing, gently, again and again. The old habit came slowly. It will leave slowly too.

That is enough for today.
