# Lesson 4: You Are Not Your Mind

In the last lesson, we saw one thing clearly. You are not your body.

So now a new thought may rise up. You might think, "Fine. I am not the body. Then surely I must be the mind. I am my thoughts. I am my feelings."

Let us look at this one too.

Watch your mind for a moment. Thoughts come and go all day long. A happy thought arrives, and then it leaves. A sad thought arrives, and then it leaves. A worry appears, stays a while, and drifts off. The mind never stops moving.

Now ask yourself one quiet question. Who sees these thoughts?

You do. You see the happy thought come, and you see it go. You see the sad thought come, and you see it go. There is someone here, watching all of it.

And if you can watch your thoughts, then you cannot be the thoughts. You are the one watching them. A thought is something you *see*. It is not what you *are*.

It is the same with your feelings. Anger comes and goes. Fear comes and goes. But you are still here after each one has left. You do not come and go along with them. You stay, and you watch.

So here is today's idea:

**You are not your mind. You are the one who watches your thoughts and feelings.**

Just see it once. The mind keeps changing, and you do not. You are behind the mind, quietly watching it.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we look more closely at this watcher. We have found that you are not the body, and not the mind. So who is it that sees them both?
