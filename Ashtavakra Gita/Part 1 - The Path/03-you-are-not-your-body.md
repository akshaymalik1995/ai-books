# Lesson 3: You Are Not Your Body

In the last lesson, we found something important. The thing that stops you is a wrong idea inside you. Today we begin to look at that idea.

So let me ask you a simple question. Who are you?

Most people answer the same way, without even thinking. They point to the body and say, *"This is me."* They feel, "I am this body. When it is young, I am young. When it is sick, I am sick. And when it dies, I am gone."

This is the first wrong idea — the idea that you are the body.

Ashtavakra says it very simply. You are not made of earth, water, fire, or air. That is only stuff. You are the one who watches the body; you are not the body itself.

Look closely with me. When you were a small child, you had a small body. That body is gone now. It changed, it grew, it became a whole new size and shape. The body you had back then is not the body you have today.

And yet, through all of it, *you* were there. The small child was you. The person reading this now is you. The body has changed many times over, but the one who says "me" never went away.

So you are not the body. The body is something you *have*. It is not what you *are*. The body keeps changing. You are the one who has it.

Here is today's idea:

**You are not your body. You are the one who has this body.**

You do not have to feel this fully yet. Just see it once. The body comes and goes and changes, and you remain. So you cannot be only the body.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we look at the next wrong idea. If you are not the body, then perhaps you are the mind? We will see.
