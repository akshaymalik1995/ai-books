# Sources

## Translation used

All verses quoted in these lessons come from:

**Ashtavakra Gita — Sanskrit text with English Transliteration & Translation**,
translated by **John Richards**.

The John Richards translation was released by the translator for free
distribution (public domain). The full PDF is kept in this folder:
`Ashtavakra Gita - Sanskrit text with English transliteration, Translation by John Richards.pdf`

## How we cite

- A citation like **1.6** means **Chapter 1, Verse 6**.
- The Gita has **20 chapters** (about 298 verses). Chapter 1 has 20 verses.
- Each lesson names the verse or verses it stands on, quoted at the top.
- The epigraph also names the **speaker** (*Janaka:* or *Ashtavakra:*), because
  the Gita is a dialogue. In Chapter 1, only verse 1.1 is Janaka (his
  question); from 1.2 on, Ashtavakra is speaking.
- If a lesson cannot be tied to a verse, that is our signal we have drifted,
  and we cut or rework it. See `verse-map.md` for the running map.

## Note on wording

We quote the John Richards translation as it stands. We do not change the
verse text. Our simple, plain-English teaching comes *after* the verse, as a
bridge to it — never as a replacement for it.
