# Lesson 4: You Are Not Your Mind

In the last lesson, we saw one thing. You are not your body.

Now a new thought may come. You may think, "Fine. I am not the body. Then I
must be the mind. I am my thoughts. I am my feelings."

Let us look at this too. Here is what Ashtavakra says:

> *Ashtavakra:* "You do not belong to the brahmin or any other caste, you are
> not at any stage, nor are you anything that the eye can see. You are
> unattached and formless, the witness of everything — so be happy."
>
> — Ashtavakra Gita 1.5

So what is he saying? You are not any of the labels put on
you — not your caste, not your role, not your stage in life. And you are not
anything that can be seen. You are the formless one who witnesses everything.
Now think about your thoughts and feelings. You can see them. So they too are
among the things you witness. They are not you.

Watch your mind for a moment. Thoughts come and go all day. A happy thought
comes. Then it goes. A sad thought comes. Then it goes. A worry comes. Then
it goes. The mind never stops changing.

Now ask yourself one thing. Who sees these thoughts?

You do. You see the happy thought come. You see it go. You see the sad
thought come. You see it go. There is someone watching all of it.

If you can watch your thoughts, then you are not the thoughts. You are the
one who watches them. A thought is something you *see*. It is not what you
*are*.

It is the same with feelings. Anger comes, then goes. Fear comes, then goes.
But you are still here after each one leaves. You do not come and go with
them. You stay and watch.

So here is the idea for today:

**You are not your mind. You are the one who watches your thoughts and
feelings.**

See it just once. The mind keeps changing. You do not. You are behind the
mind, watching it.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will look closer at this watcher. We have found that
you are not the body and not the mind. So who is the one who sees them both?
