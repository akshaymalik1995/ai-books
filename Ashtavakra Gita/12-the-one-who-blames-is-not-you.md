# Lesson 12: The One Who Blames Is Not You

In the last lesson, we saw the fight inside. When you procrastinate, one part
escapes, one part blames, one part pushes. Today we look closely at the
loudest one. The one who blames.

Go back to the scene. You are scrolling, and the voice starts. "What is wrong
with you? You always do this. You are going to fail." It is sharp. It is
sure. It knows just where to hurt.

Now I want to ask you one simple question about that voice. Did you choose to
say it?

Look and see. You did not sit and decide, "Now I will call myself a failure."
The voice just came. On its own. You did not plan it. You did not want it. It
arrived by itself, and it started hitting you.

Here is the important part. You can hear that voice. And the one who hears a
voice is not the voice. If you can hear the blaming, then you are the one
listening to it. You are not the blaming itself.

Look at it one more way. If the blaming voice were really you, you could stop
it whenever you wanted. But you cannot. It comes even when you beg it to stop.
It comes when you are already low. A thing that comes on its own, a thing you
cannot stop, cannot be you. It is something passing through you. And you are
the one who sees it pass.

This is a big turn, so go slow. All your life, you thought the blamer was
you. You thought it was you being honest. You thought it was you facing the
hard truth about yourself. But it is not you. It is just a voice. An old, hard
voice that rises on its own. You are the quiet one who hears it.

So here is the idea for today:

**The voice that blames you is not you. It comes on its own. You cannot stop
it by force. You are only the one who hears it.**

See what this gives you. If the blamer is not you, then you do not have to
believe it. And you do not have to fight it either. You can just hear it, let
it be a voice, and not take it as the truth about who you are.

That is enough for today.

In the next lesson, we will turn to the other fighter. The one who pushes
you. This one is harder to see. Because it does not feel like an enemy. It
feels like the good one, the one who is on your side.
