# Lesson 15: Understanding Comparison

Let us take everything we have learned and apply it to something the phone brings to us many times a day: comparing ourselves to other people.

Imagine this. You are scrolling on your phone. You see someone from your school or your work. They have a new job, or a happy holiday, or a life that looks perfect. And a heavy feeling settles in you.

A voice speaks in your mind.

*"They have it so much better than me. Why not me? What is wrong with my life?"*

You feel smaller than you did a minute ago. A moment before, you were fine. Now you feel that you are not enough.

Now bring in what you have learned. This is a movement of the mind. The mind has looked at another person, measured you against them, and decided you are less. That measuring is something the mind does. It rose up on its own. You did not choose it. And if it came on its own, then it is not you. It is something you can watch.

So try this. Instead of sinking into the feeling, simply notice it.

*"Comparison is here."*

Watch the thought that says you are less. See it clearly. And notice something important: the one who is watching is not less than anyone. The feeling of "not enough" is a thought in the mind. It is not the truth about you.

And see one more thing. The mind is not even comparing fairly. It takes the worst of what you feel inside — your worries, your tired days — and holds it up against the best of what another person shows on a screen. That is not a real measure. But even this is not the deepest point. The deepest point is simpler. The measuring itself is only a habit of the mind. You are the quiet one who watches it, and you never needed to compare at all.

In that watching, a little space opens. And in that space, you come back to your own life, just as it is. The comparison is still there, but you are no longer sinking into it.

Let me be honest with you. This does not mean the comparing will stop for good. The phone will show you another life tomorrow, and the feeling may rise again. But when you can watch it instead of believing it, it loses its hold. It rises, you watch, and it passes. And you are still here, complete as you are.

So here is today's idea:

**Comparison is a movement of the mind. The feeling that you are not enough is only a thought, not the truth about you. When it rises, do not believe it and do not fight it. Just watch it. The one who watches was never less than anyone.**

The next time the phone leaves you feeling small, do not scold yourself, and do not sink into it. Just watch. *"Comparison is here."* See that it is only a thought. And notice the quiet one who is watching. That one is you, and that one lacks nothing.

That is enough for today.
