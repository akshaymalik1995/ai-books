# 2. Work Without Stain

> Doing your work in this world, wish to live a hundred years.
> For you there is no other way. Done so, work does not stick to the one who does it.
>
> — Isha Upanishad, verse 2

This verse does not send me to a cave. It tells me to live long and keep working.

Work itself was never the problem.

What sticks to me is not the work — it is the claim: *I did this, so it is mine.*

When everything is already His, my work is just His world moving through my hands.

A hundred years of such work leaves no mark, the way water leaves no mark on air.

So I do not have to choose between the world and peace.

I work, fully, and own nothing of it — and that is the whole art.
