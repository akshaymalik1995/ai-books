# 18. Lead Us by the Good Path

> O Fire, you know all our deeds.
> Lead us by the good path to the goal.
> Take from us all crooked wandering.
> To you we offer our fullest words of praise.
>
> — Isha Upanishad, verse 18

The Upanishad that began with the whole world ends with a small, humble asking.

You know all our deeds — nothing hidden, nothing dressed up. The prayer begins in honesty.

Lead us by the good path — the straight one, not the clever shortcuts I invent.

Take the crookedness from us — I do not even claim I can straighten myself.

After eighteen verses of the highest truth, the last word is not "I know." It is "lead us."

Knowing I am He, and still bowing — these two do not cancel each other. Together they are the whole teaching.

The book closes. The walking begins.
