# 15. The Golden Lid

> The face of truth is covered with a golden lid.
> Remove it, O Sun, so that I, a lover of truth, may see.
>
> — Isha Upanishad, verse 15

Here the tone changes. Teaching becomes prayer.

What hides the truth from me is not darkness. It is gold.

The world is so bright, so beautiful, so rewarding, that I stop and stare at the lid itself.

Money shines, praise shines, pleasure shines — and every shining thing says: look no further.

A dark cover I would tear away at once. A golden one I polish.

So the seeker does not pray for more brightness. The seeker prays for the brightness to step aside.

And notice: I cannot lift this lid myself. Where my hands stop, asking begins.
