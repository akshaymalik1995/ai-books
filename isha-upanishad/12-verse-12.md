# 12. The Formless and the Formed

> Into blinding darkness go those who worship the unmanifest.
> Into darkness still deeper go those who delight in the manifest alone.
>
> — Isha Upanishad, verse 12

The manifest is everything with a name and form — the world I can touch.

The unmanifest is the formless source it all comes from.

Worship only the world, and I drown in things.

Worship only the formless, and I float away from life into a pale abstraction.

The verse uses the same two darknesses as before, because the mistake has the same shape: taking half of reality and calling it the whole.

The Lord of verse one wears the world as clothing. Form and formless are not rivals.

To reject either half is to tear one cloth in two and complain that both pieces are torn.
