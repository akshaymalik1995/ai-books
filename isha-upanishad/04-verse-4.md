# 4. Faster Than the Mind

> The Self is one. It does not move, yet it is swifter than the mind.
> The senses cannot catch it, for it is always ahead of them.
> Standing still, it outruns all that run.
>
> — Isha Upanishad, verse 4

My mind is the fastest thing I know. It reaches the moon in an instant.

Yet wherever the mind arrives, the Self is already there.

Not because it travels faster — because it never had to travel.

What is everywhere has nowhere to go.

The mind runs; the Self is the ground it runs on.

So I cannot catch the Self by thinking harder or searching farther.

I stop running, and find that what I was chasing never moved.
