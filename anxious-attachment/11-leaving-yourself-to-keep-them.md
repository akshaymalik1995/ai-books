# 11. Leaving Yourself to Keep Them

> Preferences are dropped before they are stated.

Plans, opinions, needs and friendships get adjusted to keep the connection smooth.

Anger is not shown, because anger carries a risk of distance.

Over time one's own position becomes unclear even to oneself.

A simple question — what do you want — becomes difficult to answer.

This is not generosity. It is a safety operation.

The relationship then holds only one person's shape.

The fear of being left has produced the disappearance it was trying to prevent.
