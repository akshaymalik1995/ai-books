# 8. The Shadow Is Not Evil, Only Exiled

> The shadow is not your evil half. It is your exiled half.

The word "shadow" sounds sinister, so hear this clearly: exile is not a verdict.

What got exiled was whatever your world could not accept — and worlds are often wrong.

A child punished for anger exiles the anger. But anger holds the power to say no.

A child mocked for crying exiles the softness. But softness holds the power to love.

A family that fears standing out teaches the child to exile their own brightness.

So the shadow is full of gold as well as mud. Jung insisted on this.

Buried aggression carries drive. Buried selfishness carries the ability to rest.

Even the mud is information: it shows exactly where you were bent to fit.

This is why meeting the shadow, though uncomfortable, makes people more alive, not worse.

What was exiled was not the worst of you. It was the rest of you.
