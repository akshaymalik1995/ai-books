# 2. The Mind You Know Is a Small Lit Room

> Consciousness is a small lit room inside a large dark house.

Everything you are aware of right now — this page, your thoughts, the feeling in your body — is inside the lit room.

The room feels like the whole house, because light is all it can see.

But the house is larger. Most of what you are stands in the dark, outside the doorway.

Forgotten years are out there. Abilities you never used. Feelings you never let in.

The dark part is not empty and not asleep. It thinks in its own way, and it acts.

It sends things into the room: a mood with no reason, a name on the tip of the tongue, a dream.

You cannot walk into the dark and switch it on. By definition, what you are aware of is the room.

But you can watch what comes through the doorway. That is how the dark house is studied.

Jung spent his life at that doorway. This map is what he found.
