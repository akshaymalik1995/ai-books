# Carl Jung, Book 1: The Map of the Psyche — page map

Series: small books, one idea per page, simple English. This book gives the
whole map once; later books go deep into each region.

Format: anxious-attachment style. Each page = title, one direct line in a
block quote, then ~10 plain lines that open it. No stories, no scenes, no
jargon left unexplained. Straight statements only.

Order = descent: surface first, then deeper, then the whole.

1. Before we begin — you are larger than the part of you that knows itself
2. The mind you know is a small lit room
3. Ego — the one who says "I"
4. The ego is not the master, only the manager
5. Persona — the face you wear for others
6. The persona is useful; the danger is believing it
7. Shadow — everything you refused to be
8. The shadow is not evil, only exiled
9. Projection — what you hate in others is often your own shadow
10. The personal unconscious — your own forgotten material
11. Complexes — the sore spots that press the buttons
12. The collective unconscious — the mind you were born with
13. Archetypes — the old patterns that shape every life
14. Anima — the inner woman in a man
15. Animus — the inner man in a woman
16. The Self — the whole of you, center and circle at once
17. The ego orbits the Self, not the other way around
18. The unconscious compensates — it balances what the ego overdoes
19. Dreams — the unconscious speaking in pictures
20. Individuation — the lifelong work of becoming whole
21. Wholeness, not perfection
22. What you do not make conscious arrives as fate
23. Hold the map lightly
24. Where the series goes from here
