# 7. Shadow — Everything You Refused to Be

> The shadow is everything about you that you decided you are not.

Every persona casts a shadow. The two are made together, like light and dark.

To build "I am kind", the unkind impulses had to be pushed out of sight.

To build "I am strong", the fear went into the dark. To build "I am humble", the ambition.

Whatever did not fit the person you decided to be — it was not deleted. There is no delete in the psyche.

It was only moved out of the lit room. Jung called that dark collection the shadow.

The shadow is personal. Yours is made of exactly what you refused, so it is shaped like you, in reverse.

It does not sit quietly. It leaks — in the joke that came out too sharp, the mood after a rival's success.

The more perfect the mask, the larger the shadow behind it. The saint's shadow is bigger than the honest sinner's.

Everyone has one. Having a shadow is not a flaw; it is the price of having a character.

The only real choice is whether you know yours, or meet it by surprise.
