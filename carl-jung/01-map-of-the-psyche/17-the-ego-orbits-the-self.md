# 17. The Ego Orbits the Self, Not the Other Way Around

> You are not the center of your psyche. You are a planet that thought it was the sun.

Once, people believed the earth was the center and the sun went around it.

The ego makes the same mistake. Standing at the center of the lit room, it assumes it is the center of everything.

Jung's map says otherwise: the ego circles the Self, as the earth circles the sun.

This single reversal changes how a life reads.

The urges you cannot explain, the dreams that will not stop, the illness that forced a turn — these stop looking like malfunctions.

They begin to look like course corrections, coming from a center that sees more than you do.

The ego's task shifts too. It is not the sun. But it is the only part that can see — the eyes of the whole.

So the ego is not to be destroyed or humbled into nothing. The Self needs it, awake and cooperative.

The healthiest position, in this map, is a working relationship: the small bright "I" listening to the large dark whole.

Jung had a name for building that relationship. It comes on a later page, once you have seen how the whole actually speaks.
