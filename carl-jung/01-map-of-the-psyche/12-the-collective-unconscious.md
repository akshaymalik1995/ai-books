# 12. The Collective Unconscious — The Mind You Were Born With

> Below your forgotten life lies a mind you never lived — and share with everyone.

Here is the claim that separated Jung from every teacher before him.

Beneath the personal layer, he said, lies a deeper one that was never personal.

You were not born blank. Your body arrived with a plan — two eyes, one heart — and so did your psyche.

The mind arrives with patterns already in it: readiness for a mother, fear of the dark, awe before something greater.

No one teaches a baby to search for a face. The expectation of a face is standing there before experience arrives.

Jung noticed that myths from lands with no contact tell the same stories — the flood, the hero, the wise old one, the trickster.

He saw images in his patients' dreams that matched ancient symbols the dreamers had never seen.

His explanation: at bottom, the human psyche has one common floor, inherited like the body, the deposit of millions of years of human experience.

This layer is the collective unconscious. Personal at the surface, human at the depths.

At the surface you are yourself. At the bottom you are mankind.
