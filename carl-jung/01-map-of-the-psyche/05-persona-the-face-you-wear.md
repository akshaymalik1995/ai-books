# 5. Persona — The Face You Wear for Others

> The persona is the face you show the world, built to be acceptable.

"Persona" was the Latin word for an actor's mask. Jung chose it on purpose.

You have a work face, a family face, a face for strangers. Each one is a persona.

It is made of what your world rewards: politeness, competence, cheerfulness, toughness — whatever earns approval where you live.

You did not design it consciously. It formed, year by year, out of what was praised and what was punished.

Watch yourself answer "How are you?" with "Fine" on a bad day. That is the persona speaking.

Notice how your voice changes when you answer the phone. That change is the mask going on.

There is nothing false about having one. A surgeon needs a surgeon's face; a parent needs a steady one.

Society could not work if everyone showed everything to everyone.

The persona, then, is a tool — a real and useful piece of the psyche.

Trouble begins only when the wearer forgets it is a mask.
