# 4. The Ego Is Not the Master, Only the Manager

> The ego believes it runs the house. It only runs the room.

The ego's mistake is natural. Since it is aware of everything in the room, it assumes it is aware of everything.

It says "I decided", "I feel", "I know why I did that" — and often it is wrong.

You decide to sleep, and the mind keeps talking.

You promise to stay calm, and the anger arrives anyway.

You know the chocolate is not needed, and the hand reaches for it.

If the ego were truly the master, none of this could happen. An order given would be an order followed.

So the honest position is smaller: the ego is a manager, running one bright office in a large firm it has never fully toured.

Much of the firm's real business is decided in rooms the manager has never entered.

A good manager does not pretend otherwise. It stays alert to signals from the rest of the house.

Knowing you are not the whole is the beginning of working with the whole.
