# 3. Ego — The One Who Says "I"

> The ego is the part of you that says "I" and means it.

Inside the lit room there is a center. Jung called it the ego.

The ego is your sense of being someone — this name, this body, this history.

It is what wakes up in the morning and finds itself the same person as yesterday.

It holds your memories in one line, so that your life feels like one story.

It chooses, plans, refuses, and takes the credit and the blame.

The word is not an insult here. In this map, "ego" does not mean pride.

It simply means the "I" — the knower at the center of the room.

Without an ego there is no one to be aware, no one to remember, no one to decide.

So the ego is necessary. The mistake is only in what it believes about itself.

That mistake is the next page.
