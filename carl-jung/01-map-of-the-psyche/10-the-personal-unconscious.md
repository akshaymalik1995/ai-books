# 10. The Personal Unconscious — Your Own Forgotten Material

> The first layer of the dark house is filled with your own life.

It is time to name the dark house properly. Jung divided it into two layers.

The first layer is the personal unconscious: everything from your own life that is no longer in the lit room.

Some of it simply faded — old phone numbers, ordinary days, faces from school.

Some of it was pushed — the humiliations, the griefs, the wishes too dangerous to keep in the light.

The shadow you have just met lives here. So does everything you learned and forgot you learned.

Faded or pushed, none of it is gone. It is stored, and it is still connected.

An old song plays and a whole year comes back — the year was there all along.

You take an instant dislike to a stranger, and only later see they resemble someone who hurt you.

This layer is personal in the strict sense: no one else has yours. It is the sediment of one particular life.

Below it, Jung claimed, lies a second layer that is not personal at all.
