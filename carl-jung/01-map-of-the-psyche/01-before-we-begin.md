# 1. Before We Begin

> You are larger than the part of you that knows itself.

These pages are notes on one map: Carl Jung's map of the psyche.

Psyche is his word for the whole mind — the part that is awake and the part that is not.

The map's central claim is simple: the mind you know is not the whole mind.

There is more of you than you can see, and the unseen part is active.

It moves your moods, your slips, your dreams, your sudden likes and hates.

Jung did not ask anyone to believe this. He asked them to watch for it.

That is the way to read these pages too. Each one holds one point.

A page is finished when it is checked against your own life, not when it is read.

Where a statement does not match what you observe, set it aside.

This book gives the whole map once, briefly. The later books walk into each region.
